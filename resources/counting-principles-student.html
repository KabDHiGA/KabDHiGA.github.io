Discovering Counting Principles

Sheet 1 · Order and repetition · GDC without factorials

Name: _____________________________   Class: __________

1 · Order matters; repetition allowed

Roll a six-sided die three times. Are (1,2,3) and (3,2,1) different? Can numbers repeat? How many sequences?

GDC / rule: N = nr. Calculate 63.

2 · Order matters; no repetition

Choose a president and vice-president from A, B, C, D. Can one student hold both roles? Does swapping roles change the result?

GDC / rule: 4 nPr 2.

3 · Order does not matter; repetition allowed

Choose two scoops from four ice-cream flavours A, B, C, D. Is AA possible? Is AB the same selection as BA?

GDC / rule: (n + r − 1) nCr r; use n = 4, r = 2.

4 · Order does not matter; no repetition

Choose two students from A, B, C, D for a team. Is AB the same as BA? May a student be chosen twice?

GDC / rule: 4 nCr 2.

Discovering Counting Principles

Sheet 2 · Multiplication, AND and OR

Name: _____________________________   Class: __________

5 · Multiplication principle

Choose one of 3 shirts, one of 2 pairs of trousers and one of 2 pairs of shoes. How many complete outfits are possible?

GDC / rule: 3 × 2 × 2.

6 · AND: successive choices

A complete lunch requires one drink from 3 options AND one sandwich from 4 options. How many lunches can you make?

GDC / rule: 3 × 4.

7 · OR: alternative categories

Choose exactly one snack: one of 4 biscuit types OR one of 3 fruit types. How many options? What if categories overlap?

GDC / rule: No overlap: add. With overlap: n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B).

Decision check

Does order matter? Is repetition allowed? Does a complete outcome require BOTH choices (AND), or one of two categories (OR)? If OR, do categories overlap?
