Question 1

Compare y = sin(x) and y = 3sin(x). What changes? What stays the same?

Reveal explanationThe amplitude increases from 1 to 3. The period stays 360° and the principal axis stays y = 0.
Question 2

Compare y = 3sin(x) and y = 3sin(2x). What happens to the period?

Reveal explanationThe period decreases from 360° to 180°. A larger |b| makes the wave repeat more often.
Question 3

For y = −3sin(2x) − 2, find the amplitude, period, principal axis, maximum and minimum.

Reveal explanationAmplitude 3; period 180°; principal axis y = −2; maximum 1; minimum −5. The negative sign reflects the wave.

Remember

Amplitude = |a| • Period = 360° / |b| • Principal axis: y = d

For a = 0 or b = 0 the graph is constant, so there is no fundamental period. For a constant function, its maximum and minimum equal its constant value. No phase-shift parameter is used in this explorer.
